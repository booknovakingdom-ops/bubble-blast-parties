Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What do we need at the venue?

A flat outdoor area and access to water and power. We will confirm the details when we quote.

What is the weather policy?

Rentals follow a 38 km/h wind limit under our rental agreement. If the weather is unsafe, we will work out a new date with you.

Who runs Bubble Blast Parties?

We're a locally owned foam party company based in Bridgewater, NS, serving events across Atlantic Canada.
